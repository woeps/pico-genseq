# Agent Guidance

Before planning or implementing changes in this repository, consult these product-direction documents:

- [Project Roadmap](ROADMAP.md) — defines the approved milestone order, outcomes, scope boundaries, and dependencies.
- [GenSeq v1 Product Charter](docs/product/genseq-v1-product-charter.md) — defines the approved product vision, target performer, core performance loop, interaction model, product principles, and non-goals.

Treat the product charter as the authority for product intent and the roadmap as the authority for sequencing major areas of work. Detailed requirements and designs may refine implementation choices, but should not silently contradict these documents.

A roadmap milestone marked complete may represent a completed planning or decision gate; it does not necessarily mean that all associated firmware capabilities have been implemented. Check the milestone outcome and linked documentation before making that assumption.
